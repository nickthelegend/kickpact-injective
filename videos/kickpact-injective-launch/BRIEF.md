## Intent

A premium, kinetic 60-second launch film for **Kickpact** — a self-custodial,
mobile-first World Cup prediction app on **Injective EVM**. Friends lock the same
kUSD stake on a match; the pot lives in an on-chain escrow that pays out only
what an oracle-signed final score proves, with the winner derived on-chain — no
admin key over the money. Premium fintech feel, low-hype, confident. Dark
hairline-grid look, one accent used only on the active/keyword/highlight. Not AI
slop — clean, considered motion.

## Customizations

- Karaoke subtitles: active word = dark text on the accent block (readable), not white-on-accent.
