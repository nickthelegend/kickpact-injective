# Kickpact — launch narration (locked VO)

Voice: am_michael (male, calm, assured, low-hype). Indented block per frame = spoken text.

## Hook (frame 1)

    Bet on the World Cup with friends — without trusting anyone to hold the money.

## The problem (frame 2)

    Every prediction app asks you to trust a custodian. Kickpact doesn't.

## How it works (frame 3)

    Friends lock the same stake. The pot lives in an escrow on Injective. An oracle signs the final score, and the contract derives the winner on-chain. The data decides.

## Self-custodial pools (frame 4)

    Open a pool in seconds. Your keys, your stake, held by code no one can override.

## Bluetooth and online duels (frame 5)

    Pot up in person over Bluetooth, or online with a duel code. Friends anywhere, one on-chain pot.

## Verifiable receipts (frame 6)

    Every payout is a receipt you can verify yourself, from your phone.

## Call to action (frame 7)

    Kickpact. Predict together. Trust the data, not us.
